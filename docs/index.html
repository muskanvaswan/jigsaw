Starting a puzzle

Tap +, then pick one of your photos, a picture from Files (on iPad and Mac), or one of the three paintings that come with the app. Choose anything from 12 to 2,000 pieces and tap Start. On a Mac you can also drag a picture onto the window.

Moving around the table

  • Drag pieces anywhere. Pinch to zoom, and drag an empty part of the table to pan.
  • On a Mac, scroll to pan and pinch on the trackpad to zoom; with a mouse, hold ⌘ and scroll to zoom.
  • Double-tap to zoom in on a spot, and again to see everything.
  • The ⋯ menu spreads loose pieces out so none overlap, or heaps them back up, and turns Snap to Board on or off.

Your puzzles on all your devices

Sign in with the round button at the top of your puzzles, and every puzzle saves to your iCloud as you play, so you can carry on from your iPhone, iPad or Mac. All your devices need to be signed in to the same iCloud account in Settings.

Short on iCloud space? Turn off Sync with iCloud in the same place. Your puzzles are taken out of iCloud and stay on each device.

Solving together

Choose Solve Together… from a puzzle's menu to invite friends and family through Messages, Mail or a link. You can solve at the same time and watch each other's pieces move, or take turns. Choose People… to see who's in or stop sharing.

Deleting puzzles

Touch and hold a puzzle in your library and choose Delete. It's removed from all your devices, and from everyone you shared it with.
