Privacy Policy

Jigsaw – Puzzle Together · Last updated 1 November 2026

Jigsaw doesn't collect any information about you. There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and nothing you do in the app is sent to us.

What stays on your device

The pictures you turn into puzzles, where every piece is, and how long you've played are saved on your device.

iCloud

If you sign in, your puzzles are also kept in your own iCloud account so they follow you to your other devices. This uses Apple's iCloud, under your Apple Account; we can't see or reach anything stored there. You can switch sync off in the app at any time, which removes your puzzles from iCloud and keeps them on your devices.

Signing in

Sign in with Apple is optional. It gives the app an anonymous identifier and, the first time, your name, which is shown to people you solve puzzles with. Your name is kept on your device and in your iCloud. Signing out removes it from the device.

Solving together

When you invite someone to a puzzle, Apple's iCloud shares that puzzle's picture, piece positions and your name with the people you invite, and theirs with you. You choose who is invited and can stop sharing at any time.

Children

Jigsaw is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone, including children.
