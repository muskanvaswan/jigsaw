## Description

Jigsaw turns any picture into a real jigsaw puzzle, with nothing on screen but the pieces and the table.

Choose one of your photos, or one of three classic paintings, pick anything from 12 to 2,000 pieces, and start. Every piece is cut its own shape, so it only fits where it belongs.

• Drag pieces anywhere and build your own heaps around the board
• Spread the loose pieces out so none overlap, or sweep them back into a heap
• Snap pieces into the board, or turn that off so they only lock to their neighbours
• Zoom and pan freely across big puzzles
• A quiet clock keeps track of your time

SOLVE TOGETHER
Invite friends and family to any puzzle. Solve it at the same time and watch each other's pieces move, or take turns whenever suits you.

YOUR PUZZLES, EVERYWHERE
Sign in and your puzzles follow you between iPhone, iPad and Mac through your own iCloud. Every move saves as you go, so you can always pick up where you left off. Short on iCloud space? Switch sync off and puzzles stay on each device.

No ads. No tracking.

## Review notes

Sign in with Apple is optional. Without it, everything works on the device. Signing in turns on iCloud sync between the reviewer's devices and Solve Together (sharing a puzzle through iCloud). No demo account is needed.

The three paintings that ship with the app are public-domain works from Wikimedia Commons: The Starry Night (Van Gogh), The Great Wave off Kanagawa (Hokusai) and Water Lilies (Monet).
